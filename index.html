About

I'm a PhD researcher at the Gran Sasso Science Institute, working on rare-event searches and the direct detection of dark matter — arguably the most fundamental unsolved problem in modern physics. I'm a member of the DarkSide collaboration, working on the DarkSide-20k experiment deep under the Gran Sasso mountain.

I completed my M.Sc. in Physics at Sapienza University of Rome (110/110), and previously worked on the CYGNO experiment for directional dark matter searches. When I'm not doing physics you'll usually find me trekking or playing the guitar.
